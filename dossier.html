The Tape Log Nine television game shows across fourteen years, three of them won. This August edition lists eight; Starface (GSN, 8 September 2006, won $900) was confirmed after it was written.

Gordon has been saying for years that he does game shows. The record was never written down. This is it: every appearance located, dated, and checked against tape, network chyrons, or his own contemporaneous words. He puts his own lifetime count at eleven, so treat eight as the floor and not the ceiling. The screen record, the quote bank, and the two long-standing mysteries are below.

01

The Weakest Link

Lost

NBC. Season 1, Episode 23. Aired 30 July 2001. Host Anne Robinson.

The one that started it. TheTVDB lists the contestant roster verbatim: Gordon Bellamy, Angela Ketterman, Pam Burns, William Blank, Daniel Robie, Debra Islam, Woogie Ham, and Ellen Fulton compete. His own contestant card read "Gordon, 30, Business Development, Video Games."

"Yeah my first was The Weakest Link and then I've done 1 show every year or so ever since."Gordon, Facebook message, 19 October 2015

The episode video he posted in 2015 has since been taken down. The roster listing and his own posts are what remain.

02

Taboo

Lost

TNN. 2002. Host Chris Wylde. Based on the Hasbro board game.

Confirmed two ways: his own photograph of himself on the TNN set with the GORDON nameplate, and the full episode preserved on YouTube, where he plays in the blue shirt.

03

Russian Roulette

Won $3,950

Game Show Network. Season 1, Episode 2. Aired 4 June 2002. Host Mark L. Walberg.

Four players, six trapdoors, and a floor that opens under whoever misses. He won the main game with $2,450, then took three of five Killer Questions at $500 each for $3,950 total, and dropped when the clock beat him on the Seven Summits. The published episode guide reads Ep 2, 6/4/02, Darren (1) Amy (3) Gordon ($3,950) Julie (2), and the tape confirms every number. He is the only Gordon in the show's entire two season run.

The full 21 minute episode is free on the Internet Archive.

04

Merv Griffin's Crosswords

Lost

Syndicated. Aired 18 September 2008. Host Ty Treadway.

Booked for 14 May 2008, then bumped. The date is nailed down by a small comedy in his inbox: a friend dutifully recorded the original date and came up empty.

Kyle: "um... i looked for you on crossword today... i dvr'd it... but no Gordon. WTF?"
Gordon: "My episode got pushed to sept 18"Facebook messages, both 14 May 2008

That puts his episode in the show's final weeks, since the series ended in September 2008. No tape has surfaced.

05

Trivial Pursuit: America Plays

Lost

Syndicated. Taped Wednesday 3 September 2008. First aired Tuesday 23 September 2008. Host Christopher Knight.

The most precisely dated appearance in the whole record, because he announced every step of it.

"on Tuesday 9/3 I tape another Game Show! Trivial Pursuit: America Plays with host Christopher Knight (yes, Peter Brady)"Gordon, Facebook, 27 August 2008

"I am On the tv, TODAY, wednesday, and if you go to tpamericaplays.com..."Gordon, Facebook, 23 September 2008

It then ran and ran. Repeats on FOX on 2 and 3 December 2008 ("is already in repeats!"), and back again on 26 March 2009 at 3:00pm on Fox 11 in Los Angeles. Syndicated, so times moved by market.

One lasting consequence: he met Chad Jones at this taping, and Chad was on the six-man team with him on Million Dollar Mind Game three years later. The circuit is how the circuit gets built.

Three weeks after the December repeat he opened Christmas at his in-laws and was handed "a DVD of my recent game show." No online copy was located in the documented search. If that disc survives, it is the only known recording of it.

06

Million Dollar Mind Game

Lost $600,000

ABC. Episodes 2 and 3, aired 30 October and 6 November 2011. Host Vernon Kay. Team: the Video Game Geeks.

The big one, and the most painful. Six players in black tie at a round table, playing the American version of the Russian format What? Where? When? ABC's own lower third introduces him as GORDON, Video Game Developer.

The team ran to $360,000 without spending a single help, then banked $600,000. Five of the six voted to take it. One teammate voted to play on, which under the rules forces the whole team to continue. They missed the next two questions and walked away with nothing. Buzzerblog later named it the number one most memorable game show moment of 2011.

It started life as a Merv Griffin Entertainment project: his 2010 callback letter invites the team to audition for a new ABC prime time game show called "6 Minds."

Both episodes are on YouTube: part one and part two.

07

The Chase

Lost

Game Show Network. Aired 26 August 2014. Host Brooke Burns, chaser Mark Labbett.

"I am back in the game on @GSN tonight. @TheChaseUSA 8/7c #HarvardCalled"Gordon, Twitter and Facebook, 26 August 2014

He had been chasing it for months. In May 2014: "AND I have to get my Quiztix on (shhhhh) I got a call for The Chase next week." Years later the episode reached a wider audience than any of the others: "I'm on Netflix on The Chase."

08

Celebrity Name Game

Won $2,200

Syndicated. Taped May 2015, aired 19 October 2015. Host Craig Ferguson. Celebrities Tom Arnold and Betsy Brandt.

He played it as a wedding present. His partner on the show was Chad Concelmo, who wanted to be on a game show, so Gordon got them both cast. The on-screen total reads CHAD & GORDON, $2,200.

"this one was fun. I did it as a wedding present for my friend, he wanted to be on a game show"Gordon, Facebook message, 19 October 2015

He also coined the best line about the experience: "dealing with Post Celebrity Name Game stress disorder."

Solved

Two mysteries that have been open for years.

Solved, in his own words, 2007 The Six Feet Under walk-on is Season 3, Episode 3. He wrote it out in a 2007 Facebook message: "Season 3, Episode 3. I was a student in Claire's class in LAC Arts. If you remember, when she was telling Ben about her new bf from the crematorium, before her and Ben were dating, I was right THERE, drawing away. Long story medium, it was our favorite show, and so I parlayed my game show winnings into an auction." The Russian Roulette money bought a walk-on at an Outfest charity auction. That is the whole chain: trapdoor to queer film festival to HBO.
Solved, 2007 Cooking with Kay is Joe's film. The unexplained 2007 producer credit on his IMDb headline is Joe Heally's movie. Gordon, November 2007: "He made a movie this year too, 5 festivals so far, www.cookingwithkay.com." It was never a mystery project. It was his husband's.

How this was built. Three source layers, in order of weight: surviving tape, checked frame by frame; network graphics and published episode guides; and Gordon's own dated messages and posts. Where only his word exists, the entry says so. No viewership estimates appear anywhere, because measured numbers for his segments do not exist.

The archive that broke it open. Most of the new material came from Gordon's own Facebook export, 17,358 posts and 544,563 messages going back to 2005. The game show record was sitting in his outbox the whole time.
