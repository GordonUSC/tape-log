# The Tape Log

Gordon Bellamy has been a contestant on eight television game shows across fourteen years. He won two.

This is that record, built as a game show. You can play his actual rounds.

**Live:** https://gordonusc.github.io/tape-log/

## What is here

- **`index.html`** — the playable version. Three rounds built from the surviving tapes:
  - **The Killer Questions.** His real *Russian Roulette* bonus round (GSN, 4 June 2002). Sixty second clock, five lights, the five questions transcribed frame by frame off the tape. He got three. See how you do.
  - **Taboo.** The fourteen real cards from his 2002 TNN episode, in broadcast order. Best played out loud with someone in the room.
  - **The Mind Game.** Five real questions from *Million Dollar Mind Game* (ABC, 2011), on the money ladder. After each answer you see the tweet Gordon actually posted at that dollar figure while the episode aired. It ends on the real $600,000 vote.
- **`dossier.html`** — the reference version. The full written record, show by show, with the verification chain for each.

## How it was verified

Three source layers, in order of weight: surviving tape checked frame by frame; network graphics and published episode guides; and Gordon's own dated posts and messages. Where only his word exists, the page says so. No viewership estimates appear anywhere, because measured numbers for his segments do not exist.
